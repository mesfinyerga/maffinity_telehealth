# TeleHealth Platform - User Manual

## Patient Mobile Application User Guide

### Getting Started

Welcome to the TeleHealth Platform Patient App! This guide will help you navigate and use all the features available to manage your healthcare needs.

#### Creating Your Account

1. **Download and Install**: Open the patient app in your web browser or mobile device
2. **Registration**: Tap "Create Account" on the login screen
3. **Personal Information**: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number
4. **Password**: Create a strong password with at least 8 characters
5. **Verification**: Verify your email address and phone number
6. **Profile Setup**: Complete your medical profile with basic health information

#### Language Selection

The app supports both English and Amharic:
- Tap the language button (🌐) at the top of any screen
- Select "English" or "አማርኛ (Amharic)"
- All text and AI responses will switch to your selected language

### Home Dashboard

Your home screen provides a quick overview of your health status and upcoming activities:

- **Health Score**: Your overall health rating based on recent activities
- **Upcoming Appointments**: Next scheduled consultations
- **Recent Activity**: Your latest health check-ins and consultations
- **Quick Actions**: Fast access to common features
- **Weather**: Local weather information for Addis Ababa

### AI Health Assistant

The AI Health Assistant helps you understand your symptoms and provides health guidance.

#### Using the AI Chatbot

1. **Access**: Tap "AI Health" in the bottom navigation
2. **Describe Symptoms**: Type or speak your symptoms in detail
3. **Provide Context**: Include when symptoms started, severity, and any triggers
4. **Review Analysis**: The AI will provide:
   - Urgency level (Low, Medium, High)
   - Possible conditions
   - Recommended actions
   - When to seek medical care

#### Understanding AI Responses

- **Green (Low Priority)**: Self-care measures may be appropriate
- **Yellow (Medium Priority)**: Consider scheduling a routine appointment
- **Red (High Priority)**: Seek medical attention promptly

**Important**: AI recommendations are for guidance only. Always consult healthcare professionals for medical decisions.

### Booking Appointments

#### Finding Healthcare Providers

1. **Browse Doctors**: Tap "Appointments" in the bottom navigation
2. **Filter Options**: Use filters for:
   - Specialty (General Medicine, Cardiology, etc.)
   - Language preference
   - Appointment type (Video or In-person)
   - Available dates and times
3. **Provider Profiles**: View doctor credentials, specializations, and patient reviews

#### Scheduling Your Appointment

1. **Select Provider**: Choose your preferred healthcare provider
2. **Choose Date/Time**: Pick from available appointment slots
3. **Appointment Type**: Select video consultation or in-person visit
4. **Reason for Visit**: Briefly describe your health concern
5. **Confirmation**: Review details and confirm your booking

#### Managing Appointments

- **View Upcoming**: See all scheduled appointments on your dashboard
- **Reschedule**: Change appointment time up to 24 hours in advance
- **Cancel**: Cancel appointments with appropriate notice
- **Reminders**: Receive notifications before your appointments

### Video Consultations

#### Preparing for Your Consultation

1. **Technical Check**: Ensure stable internet connection
2. **Environment**: Find a quiet, private space
3. **Documents**: Have relevant medical records ready
4. **Questions**: Prepare a list of questions for your doctor

#### During the Consultation

1. **Join Call**: Tap "Join Consultation" when it's time
2. **Camera/Audio**: Enable camera and microphone when prompted
3. **Screen Sharing**: Doctor may share medical images or documents
4. **Chat**: Use text chat for additional questions or information
5. **File Sharing**: Share photos or documents as requested

#### After the Consultation

- **Summary**: Review consultation notes and recommendations
- **Prescriptions**: Access any prescribed medications
- **Follow-up**: Schedule additional appointments if needed
- **Feedback**: Rate your consultation experience

### Health Records

#### Viewing Your Medical History

- **Consultations**: Complete history of all consultations
- **AI Analyses**: Previous AI health assessments
- **Prescriptions**: Current and past medications
- **Test Results**: Laboratory and imaging results
- **Allergies**: Documented allergies and reactions

#### Sharing Information

- **Export Data**: Download your health records in PDF format
- **Share with Providers**: Grant access to specific healthcare providers
- **Emergency Access**: Designate emergency contacts for medical information

### Profile and Settings

#### Personal Information

- **Basic Details**: Update name, contact information, and demographics
- **Medical History**: Add chronic conditions, surgeries, and family history
- **Medications**: List current medications and supplements
- **Allergies**: Document known allergies and reactions
- **Emergency Contacts**: Add family members or friends for emergencies

#### App Preferences

- **Notifications**: Choose which alerts you want to receive
- **Language**: Switch between English and Amharic
- **Theme**: Select light or dark mode
- **Privacy**: Manage data sharing preferences

### Privacy and Security

#### Data Protection

- Your health information is encrypted and securely stored
- Only authorized healthcare providers can access your data
- You control who can view your medical information
- All activities are logged for security purposes

#### Account Security

- Use a strong, unique password
- Enable two-factor authentication if available
- Log out when using shared devices
- Report suspicious activity immediately

### Troubleshooting

#### Common Issues

**Login Problems**:
- Check email and password spelling
- Ensure internet connection is stable
- Try password reset if needed

**AI Assistant Not Responding**:
- Check internet connection
- Try refreshing the app
- Contact support if problem persists

**Video Call Issues**:
- Test camera and microphone permissions
- Use a stable Wi-Fi connection
- Close other apps using camera/microphone

**App Running Slowly**:
- Close other apps running in background
- Restart the app
- Check available device storage

#### Getting Help

- **In-App Support**: Use the help section in settings
- **Email Support**: Contact support@telehealth.com
- **Phone Support**: Call +251-11-XXX-XXXX
- **FAQ**: Check frequently asked questions in the app
